# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Newly hired Hamilton Exteriors workers and contractors, usually opening a private signing link from WhatsApp on a phone. Some users read Spanish, some read English, and many are completing legally significant paperwork away from a desk. Hamilton's countersigner is the secondary user after the worker finishes.

## Product Purpose

Help a worker read, complete, sign, decline, or recover a legally significant employment or safety document without needing DocuSeal's admin interface. Success means the document is readable on a phone, every field remains attached to the correct signer, the worker understands the next step, and the signed record can be returned and filed without ambiguity.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a competent local contractor prepared the paperwork carefully and remains reachable if anything is unclear. Brand presence supports recognition and confidence, but never competes with the document or pressures the signer.

## Anti-references

- DocuSeal product marketing, white-label theater, or any page that promotes signing software during an employment task.
- Dense scaled-down Letter PDFs presented as the only mobile reading experience.
- Destructive decline controls styled like the primary continuation action.
- Red or pink ordinary field markers that look like validation errors.
- Decorative display typography, glass effects, gradients, oversized cards, or motion that distracts from reading and signing.
- Recovery states that strand a worker without a clear phone or WhatsApp path to a person.

## Design Principles

1. **The document is the interface.** Chrome stays quiet so the worker can read and act.
2. **Fail visibly and safely.** If responsive enhancement cannot prove every field is valid, preserve the signable PDF.
3. **Earn legal confidence.** Field ownership, required states, destructive actions, and completion copy must be unambiguous.
4. **Design for a thumb in the field.** Phone readability, touch targets, text scaling, and recovery paths are primary requirements.
5. **Keep a human within reach.** Every terminal or error state provides a direct route back to Hamilton.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across English and Spanish. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, 200% text zoom, 44 by 44 pixel touch targets, non-color state cues, readable placeholders, semantic form labels, and reliable screen-reader names. The experience must remain usable on a 390-pixel phone and must never hide the only valid document or field target.
