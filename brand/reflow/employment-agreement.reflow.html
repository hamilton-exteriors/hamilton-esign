1. Employment at will

Employment with Hamilton is at will. Either the Employee or Hamilton may end the employment at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. Nothing in this Agreement, in any handbook, or in anything said by any Hamilton representative creates a contract of employment for any fixed period or a promise of continued employment. Only the President of ABR Quality Resources Inc may change the at-will nature of employment, and only in a writing signed by both parties.

2. Position and duties

The Employee is hired in the position checked above to perform residential and commercial roofing work, including tear-off, deck repair, underlayment, shingle and flat-roof installation, cleanup, and related exterior work. Jobsites change daily and are located throughout the San Francisco Bay Area and surrounding counties. The Employee will perform the work assigned by the foreman or by Hamilton management, follow all safety rules, and report to the assigned jobsite at the assigned time.

Hours vary with the work available. Hamilton does not guarantee any minimum number of hours, days, or weeks of work.

3. Compensation

3.1 Hourly rate. The Employee is paid $16.90 per hour for every hour worked, which is the California minimum wage. If the Employee works hours in a city with a local minimum wage higher than that rate, the higher city rate applies to the hours worked in that city that day. Hamilton maintains the city wage table and applies it automatically.

3.2 Production bonus. In addition to the hourly rate, the Employee earns a nondiscretionary production bonus for each roofing square installed on a completed job:

Position Bonus per square installed
Roofer $14.90 per square
Foreman $29.90 per square

This bonus is paid in addition to the hourly rate, never in place of it. The hourly rate is the guaranteed floor and is paid for all hours worked whether or not any squares are installed that day. Squares are counted from the completed, approved job measurement.

3.3 Overtime. Overtime is paid at 1.5× the regular rate of pay for hours over 8 in a workday and over 40 in a workweek, and at 2× the regular rate for hours over 12 in a workday and for hours over 8 on the seventh consecutive day worked in a workweek. Because the production bonus is nondiscretionary, it is included in the regular rate used to calculate overtime, and overtime is trued up in the pay period the bonus is paid.

3.4 Payday. Wages are paid weekly on Friday, for the workweek running Sunday through Saturday that closed six days earlier. Wage statements are itemized as required by Labor Code §226 and show hours, rates, the production bonus, and all deductions.

3.5 Changes. Hamilton may change the rates above going forward. Any change is prospective only, never retroactive, and the Employee will receive an updated Labor Code §2810.5 wage notice within 7 days of the change or on the next wage statement.

3.6 Deductions. Hamilton makes only lawful deductions: taxes, withholdings ordered by law, and deductions the Employee authorizes in writing. Hamilton does not deduct from wages for broken or lost tools, damaged materials, vehicle damage, cash shortages, or customer non-payment.

4. Timekeeping

The Employee must record actual start time, meal period start and end, and stop time every day, and must record the city of the jobsite worked each day. Working off the clock is prohibited. Falsifying time records is grounds for discipline up to termination. If a time record is wrong, the Employee must report it before the pay period closes so it can be corrected.

5. Meal and rest periods

The Employee gets an unpaid, uninterrupted 30-minute meal period beginning before the end of the fifth hour of work, and a second 30-minute meal period before the end of the tenth hour when working more than 10 hours. The Employee gets a paid 10-minute rest period for every four hours worked or major fraction thereof. Hamilton relieves the Employee of all duty during meal periods. The Employee must take these breaks and must report to a supervisor immediately if anything prevents taking one.

6. Paid sick leave

The Employee receives California paid sick leave: a minimum of 40 hours or 5 days per year, accrued and used as provided by Labor Code §246. Hamilton does not offer accrued paid vacation or PTO beyond this sick leave. If Hamilton adds a vacation or PTO benefit in the future it will be provided in writing.

7. Tools, equipment, and expenses

Because the Employee's base rate is at the minimum wage, Hamilton supplies all tools, equipment, and personal protective equipment required for the work at no cost to the Employee, as required by Wage Order 16 §3. The Employee is not required to buy or provide tools or PPE.

Hamilton reimburses necessary business expenses under Labor Code §2802, including approved mileage when the Employee is directed to drive a personal vehicle for company business between jobsites during the workday. Ordinary commuting to the first jobsite and home from the last jobsite is not reimbursed. Expenses must be submitted with receipts.

8. Safety

Roofing is high-hazard work and safety compliance is a condition of employment. The Employee agrees to:

  1. Follow Hamilton's Injury and Illness Prevention Program, Heat Illness Prevention Plan, and fall protection program at all times.
  2. Wear and correctly use the fall protection, helmet, eye protection, gloves, and footwear Hamilton provides. Working on a roof without required fall protection is grounds for immediate termination.
  3. Report every injury, near miss, and unsafe condition to the foreman the same day it happens, no matter how minor.
  4. Attend and sign off on required safety training before working on a roof.
  5. Never work under the influence of alcohol, cannabis, or drugs that impair the ability to work safely.

The Employee has the right to refuse work they reasonably believe is unsafe, and Hamilton will not retaliate for raising a safety concern or reporting an injury.

9. Workers compensation

Hamilton carries workers compensation insurance covering the Employee. Work-related injuries must be reported the same day so a claim form can be provided within one working day. The Employee has been given the DWC Time of Hire pamphlet and the opportunity to predesignate a personal physician.

10. Driving

If the Employee drives any vehicle for Hamilton, the Employee must hold a valid California driver's license, keep it current, provide a copy, authorize a motor vehicle record check, and report any suspension or revocation immediately. No phone use while driving. Company vehicles are for company business only.

11. Company property and confidential information

Hamilton property, including tools, equipment, vehicles, keys, uniforms, and phones, must be returned on request and on the last day of employment. Customer lists, pricing, measurements, supplier pricing, and job documents are Hamilton's confidential business information and trade secrets and may not be disclosed or used for anyone other than Hamilton.

During employment, the Employee will not perform roofing or exterior work for Hamilton's customers or prospects outside of Hamilton, and will not solicit Hamilton's customers for personal work. This obligation ends when employment ends. Nothing in this Agreement restricts the Employee from working anywhere they choose after employment ends; California does not permit noncompete agreements and Hamilton does not use them.

12. Rights that cannot be waived

Nothing in this Agreement prevents the Employee from discussing wages or working conditions with coworkers, filing a claim with the Labor Commissioner, the Civil Rights Department, the EEOC, Cal/OSHA, or any other agency, cooperating in an investigation, or reporting suspected illegal conduct. Hamilton does not retaliate for any of these.

13. Equal opportunity and harassment

Hamilton is an equal opportunity employer and does not discriminate or tolerate harassment based on race, color, national origin, ancestry, religion, sex, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, age, disability, medical condition, marital status, military or veteran status, or any other protected characteristic. Report harassment or discrimination to Hamilton management immediately.
